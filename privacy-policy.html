Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

Our website address is: https://bowtiesolutionsar.com. Bow Tie Solutions is an electrical contractor serving Northwest Arkansas and surrounding areas.

What Personal Data We Collect and Why

We collect information you provide directly when you contact us through our website forms, including your name, email address, phone number, and any details about the services you're requesting. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and provide you with an estimate or service.

If you use our contact form, we may also collect your IP address and browser user agent string to help with spam detection.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your experience. If you submit a contact form, a temporary cookie may be saved to your browser to confirm your submission was received. This cookie contains no personal data and is discarded when you close your browser.

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies on this site.

Embedded Content from Other Websites

Pages on this site may include embedded content such as Google Maps. Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if you had visited those websites directly. These services may collect data about you, use cookies, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content.

Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information submitted through our contact form is used only by Bow Tie Solutions to respond to your request. If you request a password reset (where applicable), your IP address will be included in the reset email.

How Long We Retain Your Data

Contact form submissions and the personal information they contain are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill your request and for reasonable follow-up communications. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights Over Your Data

If you have contacted us or provided personal information through our website, you may request to receive a copy of the data we hold about you or ask that we erase it. To make such a request, contact us at (479) 426-4740 or through our contact page.

Where Your Data Is Sent

Contact form submissions may be routed through our CRM partner for the purpose of managing and responding to your inquiry. Visitor comments and form data may be checked through an automated spam detection service.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us or call (479) 426-4740.
