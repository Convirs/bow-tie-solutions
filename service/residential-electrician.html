Residential Electrical Services

Safe, code-compliant electrical work for your home — from new outlets to complete new-construction wiring.

Professional Residential Electrical in Arkansas

Your home's electrical system is one of its most critical — and often most overlooked — components. At Bow Tie Solutions, we handle everything from simple outlet additions and light fixture swaps to complete panel replacements and whole-home wiring on new builds. Every job is done to Arkansas code, and we carry full insurance for your protection.

Whether you're updating an older home, finishing a basement, adding a home office, or building from the ground up, our team brings over 20 years of hands-on residential electrical experience to the job. We work cleanly, communicate clearly, and never leave until you're satisfied.

  • Electrical panel upgrades and replacements
  • Outlet and switch installation (standard, GFCI, USB, 240V)
  • New construction and remodel wiring
  • Ceiling fan and light fixture installation
  • Smoke detector and carbon monoxide detector wiring
  • Home electrical inspections and safety assessments
  • Whole-home surge protection
