Commercial Electrical Services

Full-service commercial electrical for businesses of all sizes across southwest Arkansas.

Commercial Electricians in Arkansas

Running a business means your electrical system can't afford to fail. Bow Tie Solutions provides reliable commercial electrical services for retail stores, restaurants, office buildings, multi-tenant spaces, and more throughout Arkansas. We bring a detail-oriented approach to every commercial job and keep projects on time and to code.

From wiring a brand-new tenant space to upgrading lighting in an existing facility, we coordinate with contractors, building owners, and inspectors to make sure every project is completed on time and to code.

  • New tenant buildouts and commercial construction wiring
  • Commercial panel upgrades and service increases
  • LED lighting retrofits and commercial lighting systems
  • Exit and emergency lighting installation
  • Electrical code compliance inspections
  • Data and low-voltage wiring rough-in
  • Parking lot and exterior lighting
