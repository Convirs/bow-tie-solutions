EV Charger Installation Throughout Arkansas

Charging your electric vehicle at home should be fast, safe, and reliable. Bow Tie Solutions installs Level 2 EV charging stations for homeowners and businesses throughout Arkansas, replacing slow Level 1 trickle charging with a dedicated 240V circuit that adds 20–30+ miles of range per hour overnight. Every installation is permitted and code-compliant.

Whether you're adding a charger to your garage, upgrading to a smart charging station, or outfitting a commercial parking lot with multiple EV stations, we handle the full electrical scope — panel assessment, wiring, conduit, mounting, and permitting.

  • Level 2 (240V) home EV charger installation
  • Commercial EV charging stations for businesses and parking lots
  • Panel assessment and capacity upgrades if needed
  • All required permits and inspections
  • Hardwired and outlet-style installations
  • Smart charger compatibility (Tesla, ChargePoint, Enphase, and more)
  • Multi-unit and fleet charging infrastructure

Common Questions

EV Charger Installation FAQ

Level 1 charging uses a standard 120V outlet and adds roughly 3-5 miles of range per hour — suitable for plug-in hybrids or overnight charging when you drive fewer miles. Level 2 uses a dedicated 240V circuit and adds 20-30+ miles per hour, making it the recommended choice for most EV owners who drive regularly.

It depends on your current panel's available capacity. We assess your panel during the estimate visit. Many homes have enough capacity to add a 240V EV circuit without a full panel upgrade. If your panel is at capacity, we'll give you an honest recommendation on what's needed.

Most residential Level 2 EV charger installations can be completed in a single day, often in just a few hours. The timeline depends on the distance from your panel to your garage and whether any conduit work through finished walls is required.

Yes. Bow Tie Solutions installs commercial EV charging stations for businesses, parking lots, and multi-tenant properties throughout southwest Arkansas. We can install multiple charging stations and handle all associated electrical infrastructure including trenching, conduit, and panel work.
