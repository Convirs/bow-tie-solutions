Industrial Electrical Services

Three-phase power, motor controls, and heavy-duty industrial electrical for facilities throughout Arkansas.

Industrial Electricians in Arkansas

Manufacturing plants, warehouses, and industrial facilities demand a higher level of electrical expertise — and Bow Tie Solutions delivers. We handle complex industrial electrical systems including three-phase power, high-voltage service upgrades, motor controls, and machine wiring for facilities throughout Arkansas. Every job is completed to NEC and OSHA standards.

Whether you need new equipment wired, a three-phase service upgraded, or rapid troubleshooting when production goes down, our team has the experience and equipment to get it done efficiently. We work around your schedule to minimize downtime and keep your operation running.

  • Three-phase power installation and service upgrades
  • Motor controls, VFDs, and soft starters
  • Industrial machine and equipment wiring
  • Electrical panel upgrades and distribution boards
  • Conduit systems for manufacturing environments
  • Industrial electrical troubleshooting and repair
  • OSHA and NEC compliant installations
