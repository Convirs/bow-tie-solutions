Standby Generator Installation in Arkansas

Power outages in Arkansas can last hours or days — and when they hit, a whole-home standby generator keeps your lights on, your HVAC running, and your refrigerator cold without any action on your part. Bow Tie Solutions handles the complete electrical scope of generator installation, including transfer switches, panel connections, and full code-compliant wiring throughout Arkansas.

From correctly sizing the generator for your home or business to coordinating the gas line connection and passing final inspection, we manage the entire process. We work with leading generator brands and ensure every installation is safe, properly permitted, and ready for the next storm.

  • Whole-home standby generator installation
  • Automatic and manual transfer switch installation
  • Generator load calculation and sizing
  • Electrical panel connections and subpanel wiring
  • Commercial backup power systems
  • Critical load panel installation
  • Post-installation testing and commissioning

Generator Installation FAQ

Generator sizing depends on what you want to power during an outage. A 10-16kW unit typically covers essential circuits like lights, refrigerator, HVAC, and sump pump. A whole-home generator of 20kW+ can power everything simultaneously. We'll calculate your load and recommend the right size during your free estimate.

Yes. Every generator installation includes a proper transfer switch — either a manual transfer switch or an automatic transfer switch (ATS). The transfer switch safely isolates your generator from the utility line, protecting utility workers and your equipment. Never run a generator without a proper transfer switch.

Most residential standby generator installations can be completed in one to two days.
